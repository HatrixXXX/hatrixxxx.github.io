Toolbox icon sources
====================

The toolbox inventory is maintained in src/data/software-tools.ts.
The installed-software icons were copied from this computer's D: drive.
Executable icons were extracted from Windows icon resources without
running the applications. Native PNG frames are kept at 256 or 512px;
native DIB frames are kept in 256px ICO containers. The local SVGs have
512x512 intrinsic dimensions. Two source-limited PNGs remain at 128px:
SolidWorks and Q-dir. No 128px icon was merely enlarged to claim
higher source resolution. Vivado, MobaXterm, and SpaceSniffer are 512px
transparent PNGs reconstructed from their original app marks.

The local D: paths are provenance only. The website serves local PNGs
and does not depend on those installed applications at runtime. Product
marks remain trademarks of their owners.

The source matches the installed 7zFM.exe icon. Adaptation: centered its
16x12 artwork in a transparent square with the previous icon's margins
and 512x512 intrinsic size.

Reconstructed 512px marks:

Vivado: Three flat-color polygons traced from the installed icon and
rasterized at 512x512, preserving the original transparent gap.

MobaXterm: The official image is only 48x48. The 512px transparent asset
is an imagegen edit of the original app mark.

SpaceSniffer: The largest icon frame in the installed executable and the
newer official release is only 48x48. The 512px transparent asset is an
imagegen edit of that mark.

Imagegen edit prompts (built-in tool; 1254px outputs cleaned of isolated
alpha artifacts and reduced to 512px):

MobaXterm: Faithfully reconstruct the existing dark terminal panel, thin
silver-gray beveled frame, white >_ glyph, and interlocked red, blue, and
lime-green blocks. Preserve their arrangement, proportions, palette, and
dimensional shading. Improve edge clarity only. Transparent outside the
icon silhouette; no added text, symbols, shadow, glow, or redesign.

SpaceSniffer: Faithfully reconstruct the existing single golden-yellow
jigsaw piece with circular head, rounded arms, right-side notch, two short
feet, bright upper-left highlights, and amber bevel. Improve edge clarity
only. Transparent outside the piece; no text, extra pieces, shadow, glow,
or redesign.
