Toolbox icon sources
====================

The toolbox inventory is maintained in src/data/software-tools.ts.
The 28 icons in the first section were copied
from software installed on this computer's D: drive. Executable icons
were extracted from their Windows icon resources without running them.
All icons occupy 128x128 PNG canvases with transparent outer margins;
the application's own colored tile remains part of its icon.

The local D: paths are provenance only. The website serves local PNGs
and does not depend on those installed applications at runtime. Product
marks remain trademarks of their owners.
