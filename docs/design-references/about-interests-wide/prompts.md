# About interests wide backgrounds

The selected runtime images are `public/assets/about-interests/{singing,long-run,model-painting,anime}-wide.webp`, each 1920 × 240. The prompts below reconstruct their subject, framing, lighting, and exclusions from the selected images; they are not a verbatim record of the generation calls. The left half stays dark and quiet so live HTML text remains legible. The subjects occupy the right half. Cards, titles, descriptions, borders, and hover effects are rendered by the page, not baked into the artwork.

## Singing

> Use case: photorealistic panoramic card background. An empty private karaoke room at night, seen in a very wide cinematic frame. On the right, two real handheld microphones rest diagonally on a low dark marble table beside drinking glasses and small snacks; upholstered sofas, acoustic wall panels, and a distant display showing only abstract purple and cyan light complete the room. Lively magenta, violet, and blue practical lighting with believable reflections. Extend the same room naturally toward the left, progressively darker and softly out of focus, leaving broad uncluttered space for overlaid text. The microphones and room remain recognizable when the image fills a shallow 8:1 card. No people, performers, faces, lyrics, readable text, logos, watermarks, or baked-in UI.

## Long run

> Use case: photorealistic panoramic card background. At blue-hour sunrise, one runner of ordinary build moves away from the camera on the far-right side of a wet riverside path, full body and face unseen. A low stone railing traces the river toward a distant bridge and city skyline; faint mist hovers over still water, with cool slate-blue shadows and a narrow warm amber horizon. Let the landscape and water continue across the entire very wide, shallow frame. Keep the left half darker, soft, and free of prominent subjects to support overlaid text. Documentary realism, natural anatomy, quiet persistence rather than a racing scene. No other people, crowds, signs, numbers, brands, readable text, watermarks, or baked-in UI.

Behind it sit paint jars, brushes, a stained palette, and warm task lighting; physical paint, plastic/resin, and tiny sculpted details must be believable.
