# Protected gate unlock actuator: dark center plate

## Purpose and layering

The generated image replaces only the horizontally adaptable center slice of the existing unlock button. The original rigid left and right armor slices remain above it. The real button, its “解锁” label, disabled state, pointer response, and keyboard focus remain HTML and CSS. At runtime the center material stretches within a roughly 430 × 50 px desktop button and narrower mobile buttons; the center must remain quiet enough for a short light label.

## Complete prompt (one image)

> Edit image 1, the exact existing center slice of a website's physical unlock actuator. Keep its perfectly straight horizontal black/brushed-metal top and bottom rails, thin warm copper seams, framing, proportions and front-on perspective. Change only the huge flat, bright yellow central plate: make it a recessed, dark blue-gray/graphite-violet machined titanium pressure plate with realistic very fine horizontal brushing, subtle shallow layered metal planes and used industrial surface variation. Make the plate unmistakably a physical sci-fi industrial/mecha control, consistent with image 2's lock-core crown materials. Retain only narrow, restrained amber light in the top and bottom seams, and perhaps two short hairline copper traces near the far edges. No large luminous/solid yellow area and no neon glow. The central 65% must be quiet, DARK and medium contrast so a short live white Chinese label will later be overlaid in CSS. Stay symmetric, orthographic, full bleed, with clean exact parallel rails for nine-slice scaling. No text, logos, glyphs, locks, icons, people, webpage, shadow outside component or new protrusions. Image 1 is the precise layout/edit target; image 2 is a material and palette reference only.

Image 1 was `public/assets/protected-gate/industrial-release-center.webp`; image 2 was `public/assets/protected-gate/lock-core-crown.webp`. The prompt follows the existing dark titanium, copper, amber, and sparse cyan system in `DESIGN.md` and `src/styles/tokens.css`. It replaces the solid bright field without changing the selected crown or end armor.
